(3:25–3:50) The numbers. In our physics simulation, using our own arm's CAD, the arm picked and placed all 24 cubes it went for, across five random scatters, and the camera located each cube to within one or two millimetres. On the real headset, a teammate's held jaw clench came through at almost 11 times the resting level on the jaw electrode, and almost 6 times on the temple, through all that hum. What we haven't measured yet: picks on the printed arm, and end-to-end accuracy with real users. That's next. [If asked about the eye tracker: the first real profile had a median error of 229 pixels on a laptop screen. That's why we zoom in on the objects, pick the nearest one, and always confirm with a clench.]
