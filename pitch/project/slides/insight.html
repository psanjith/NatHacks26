(1:10–1:25) So here's the idea. Your eyes choose, your jaw confirms, and the robot moves. A camera turns whatever is on the table into the menu. A plain webcam follows your eyes, and the object you look at lights up. To confirm, you hold a jaw clench, which our EEG headset picks up on two electrodes. To be clear, that's a muscle signal, not a thought. We chose it because it works in a real room, for a real user. The arm does all the moving.
