OpenBCI Ganglion, 4 channels. 185 recorded trials over 3 sessions with one teammate. Full analysis in our paper.

(0:40–1:10) We started where most brain–computer interface projects start: SSVEP. You look at a flickering light, and your visual cortex echoes its frequency. We recorded 185 trials over three sessions. Our 4-channel headset picked up mains hum about a thousand times bigger than the response we were looking for, and the response itself was barely above the noise: 1.1 times, where working setups see 2 to 5. We tried every decoder we could, from the standard CCA methods to a neural net and a reinforcement-learning agent. None beat 32% on 7 targets. So we measured, and we changed course. A jaw clench and an eye movement are hundreds of microvolts, and the same headset sees them clearly.
