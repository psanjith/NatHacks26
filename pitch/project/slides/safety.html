Designed for its users

Safe by default, and never EEG-only

Safety

  • A long jaw clench or the STOP button halts the arm from any state
  • The STOP button cuts the servos in the arm’s own firmware
  • The arm freezes if the laptop goes silent for 0.5 s
  • Nothing flickers while the arm is moving

Accessibility

  • Every choice also works with one switch, or an assistant’s keyboard
  • No jaw clench needed: it can weaken in bulbar ALS
  • A flashing-light warning before any flicker
  • No time pressure: it waits until you choose
(3:50–4:15) We designed for the people who’ll actually use this. Safety never depends on EEG or on the laptop: a long clench or a physical STOP button halts the arm, and that button cuts the servos inside the arm’s own firmware; if the laptop goes quiet for half a second, the arm freezes. And it’s never EEG-only: jaw strength can fade in bulbar ALS, so every choice also works with a single switch or an assistant on a keyboard. We warn before any flashing light, and nothing flickers while the arm moves.
