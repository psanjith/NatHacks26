How to play
  1. Each player starts with six tokens. Take turns placing one token in A, B, or C.
  2. After all tokens are placed, the majority in each territory earns its 3, 4, or 5 points. Ties earn nothing.
  3. The highest total wins. The starting player switches for the next game.

For a tabletop demo, an operator stages Player 1's token at the pickup point. Player 2 places their own token. Confirm every placement before advancing.
