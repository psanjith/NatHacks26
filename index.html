How to play
  1. Players take turns placing one token in a territory.
  2. Territories are worth 3, 4, and 5 points.
  3. When all tokens are placed, the player with the most tokens in a territory earns its points.
  4. A tie for the most tokens awards no points.
  5. The highest total score wins.

The backend configures the players and the full or short game.
