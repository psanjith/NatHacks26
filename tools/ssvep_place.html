Good under 50 kΩ. Watch it drop as you press the electrode in. EEG pauses while it measures, so stop it before running the test. It can’t see the forehead ground.

What the test checks

  1. Eyes closed → alpha. Closing the eyes makes a strong ~10 Hz rhythm at the back of the head. Good spot: at least 2× stronger than eyes open.
  2. Flicker → SSVEP. A big square flashing 7.5 times a second should show up at 7.5 Hz (and 15 Hz). Good spot: at least 3× more than with no flicker.
  3. Only the visual cortex does both. If neither shows up, the electrode is too high, off to the side, or not touching skin.
